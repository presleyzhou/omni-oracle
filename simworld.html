M3 · Parallel-World Simulation Engine

Extract seed information from the real world — breaking news, draft policies, financial signals — and auto-construct a high-fidelity parallel digital world. Thousands of agents with independent personas, long-term memory and behavioral logic interact and socially evolve. Through the god view you inject variables and steer the run — let the future rehearse in a digital sandbox, so decisions win after a hundred simulated battles. Upload seed materials, describe your prediction request in natural language; Omni Oracle returns a detailed forecast report plus a deeply interactive digital world.

Pipeline

Five stages, mirroring the open-source MiroFish architecture: GraphRAG world-building → persona genesis → social simulation → report → deep interaction.

🧪 Backtest suite

Nine historical events with known outcomes, each fast-forwarded 20 times (30 simulated days). The engine's scenario call is compared to what actually happened; the sentiment path is scored by RMSE against the recorded history.

📐 Scale audit — will the mechanism survive more agents?

Before scaling a social mechanism, ask three questions (arXiv 2608.22884): how often can it fire, do agents actually use the information it supplies, and does the measurement itself create a false scale effect. The audit rebuilds the world's small-world topology at four population sizes and measures how far a message travels with the current reach and lifetime dials.

🔬 Pattern replication — AgentSociety-style validation

Large agent simulations are validated by reproducing qualitative patterns from real social experiments (AgentSociety, arXiv 2502.08691: polarization, shock response, policy effects). Two of those patterns are checked here against the engine's own dynamics.

⚖️ Resolution desk (AI pre-settlement)

Every configured model judges independently whether a market has resolved; only a unanimous, high-confidence verdict is proposed for auto-settlement — anything else is routed to human review (hybrid design that reached 97.9% accuracy on the 47% of cases it auto-resolved, arXiv 2605.30802).
